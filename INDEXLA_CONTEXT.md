 Project Overview

INDEXLA is building the next generation of decentralized portfolio management.

The mission is to become the leading platform for on-chain investing by allowing anyone to discover, create, automate, and invest in diversified portfolios while maintaining direct ownership of their assets.

Core thesis:

"Invest in Everything. Own Everything. Control Everything."

Unlike traditional portfolio management, INDEXLA combines self-custody, AI-assisted execution, cross-chain infrastructure, creator monetization, and multi-asset investing into one platform.

# Product Philosophy

INDEXLA is NOT:

- a trading bot

- an AI that predicts markets

- a custodial platform

- a copy-trading platform

INDEXLA IS:

- decentralized portfolio infrastructure

- rule-based investment automation

- creator economy for investment products

- multi-chain investment platform

- non-custodial asset management

Investors define every rule.

Automation executes those rules.

Assets always remain under user ownership.

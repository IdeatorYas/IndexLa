# INDEXLA FAQ

# 1. Investor Questions

## What is INDEXLA?

INDEXLA is a decentralized, non-custodial portfolio management platform that allows investors to build and manage multi-asset portfolios and automate predefined investment strategies across supported assets and chains.

## What problem does INDEXLA solve?

Investors increasingly have access to crypto, tokenized stocks, commodities, RWAs, and other on-chain assets, but managing them across different chains and platforms remains fragmented.

INDEXLA provides a portfolio layer that combines asset selection, allocation, strategy automation, and cross-chain execution in one interface.

## Is INDEXLA an investment fund?

No.

INDEXLA does not pool investor funds into a centralized fund and does not take custody of investor assets.

Investors create or follow portfolios while retaining control of their own assets.

## Does INDEXLA manage my money?

No.

INDEXLA provides portfolio and execution infrastructure. Investors define their assets, allocations, strategies, and execution parameters.

The platform does not take discretionary control of investor capital.

## Where are my assets held?

Assets remain in the investor's wallet or authorized account infrastructure.

Investors do not need to deposit assets into an INDEXLA-controlled custodial account.

## Can INDEXLA withdraw my funds?

No.

Execution permissions are defined by the investor and can be revoked by the investor.

INDEXLA is designed so that execution authority does not equal unrestricted custody.

## What does non-custodial mean here?

It means investors retain control of their assets rather than transferring them to INDEXLA for centralized management.

The platform can execute permitted transactions according to the conditions and limits established by the investor.

## Can I revoke execution permissions?

Yes.

Investors can revoke their permissions without requiring INDEXLA to approve or process the withdrawal.

## What assets can I invest in?

Depending on supported integrations and jurisdiction, INDEXLA is designed to support:

- Crypto assets
- Tokenized equities
- Commodities
- Tokenized real-world assets
- Other supported on-chain financial exposures

Asset availability depends on liquidity, infrastructure, issuer availability, and regulatory requirements.

## Can I combine different asset classes?

Yes.

A portfolio can combine supported assets across different categories.

For example:

BTC + SOL + tokenized equities + gold exposure

The objective is to allow investors to manage multiple exposures through one portfolio rather than separate platforms.

## Is INDEXLA itself an index token?

No.

INDEXLA's core product is a portfolio management and execution layer.

Investors own the underlying supported assets rather than receiving a single token that merely represents a basket.

## Why not simply buy an index token?

Index products can simplify diversification, but they can also reduce control over individual positions and allocations.

INDEXLA is designed around direct ownership and programmable portfolio control.

Investors choose the underlying assets, allocations, and strategy parameters.

## Does INDEXLA guarantee returns?

No.

INDEXLA does not guarantee investment performance.

Strategies can lose money, and historical performance does not guarantee future results.

## Is INDEXLA financial advice?

No.

INDEXLA provides infrastructure for portfolio construction and execution.

It does not provide personalized financial, investment, or tax advice.

Investors remain responsible for determining whether an asset or strategy is appropriate for them.

## Why automate investment decisions?

The objective is consistency.

Investors often know what they want to do but hesitate when markets become volatile.

Automation allows predefined decisions to be executed according to rules instead of emotion, FOMO, panic, or missed opportunities.

## Who decides what I buy?

The investor.

The investor selects the assets, allocations, strategy, thresholds, and execution parameters.

INDEXLA does not independently decide what an investor should buy.

## What does the AI do?

AI monitors conditions defined by the investor and coordinates execution when those predefined conditions are met.

The AI can monitor markets, evaluate strategy conditions, coordinate execution infrastructure, and handle execution workflows.

It does not replace the investor's strategy with its own discretionary investment decisions.

## Can the AI change my strategy?

No, not outside the permissions and parameters established by the investor.

The investor defines the strategy.

AI monitors and coordinates execution.

## What strategies does INDEXLA support?

Initial strategy templates include:

- Buy Fear / Sell Greed
- RSI
- Momentum
- Take Profit
- Rebalancing

Additional strategies can be introduced as the platform develops.

## Can strategies be customized?

Yes.

Investors can define parameters such as thresholds, timeframes, allocation sizes, target allocations, drift ranges, and position adjustments depending on the strategy.

## What is Buy Fear / Sell Greed?

It uses defined market-sentiment conditions to accumulate during periods of extreme fear and reduce exposure during periods of extreme greed.

The objective is not to predict the exact market bottom or top.

## What is RSI automation?

Investors can define RSI conditions that trigger accumulation or exposure reduction.

Parameters can include RSI thresholds, timeframe, and allocation size.

## What is momentum automation?

Momentum strategies can increase or decrease exposure based on predefined trend conditions.

The investor determines the relevant parameters and position adjustment.

## What is automated rebalancing?

Investors establish target allocations and acceptable drift ranges.

When allocations move outside the defined range, the strategy can trigger a rebalance.

## What is automated take profit?

Investors define a profit threshold and the percentage of a position to reduce when that threshold is reached.

## Can I stop automation?

Yes.

Investors can pause or revoke execution permissions.

## Can I manually manage my portfolio?

Yes.

Automation is optional.

Investors can manage their portfolios manually or use predefined strategies.

## Can I build my own portfolio from scratch?

Yes.

Investors can select supported assets, define allocations, and choose or configure a strategy.

## Can I follow a creator portfolio?

Yes.

Investors can discover creator portfolios through the marketplace and evaluate factors such as allocations, strategy, performance, activity, and AUM.

## Do I have to copy a creator exactly?

No.

Investors can follow a creator strategy, use its allocation as a starting point, customize it, or build an entirely independent portfolio.

## What information can investors see about creator portfolios?

Depending on the portfolio, investors can see:

- Asset allocations
- Strategy
- PnL
- Performance
- Activity
- AUM
- Creator reputation
- Verified performance history

## How are portfolios ranked?

Marketplace discovery can consider verified performance history, AUM velocity, creator reputation, and other defined marketplace metrics rather than follower count alone.

## What makes INDEXLA different from Velvet?

INDEXLA focuses on combining multi-asset investing, non-custodial portfolio ownership, rule-based automation, cross-chain execution, and creator-led distribution.

Velvet has developed AI-agent-based portfolio automation, but its architecture and product model differ from INDEXLA's rule-defined, investor-controlled portfolio approach.

## What makes INDEXLA different from Autopilot?

Autopilot focuses on automated execution around tracked investment signals and creator portfolios in traditional brokerage environments.

INDEXLA is designed around on-chain, multi-asset, cross-chain portfolio execution and self-custodial asset ownership.

## What makes INDEXLA different from SoSoValue?

SoSoValue provides investment intelligence, indexes, and trading automation.

INDEXLA focuses on turning investor-defined strategies and creator investment theses into programmable, non-custodial portfolios.

## What makes INDEXLA different from traditional asset managers?

Traditional asset managers generally control or manage portfolios on behalf of clients and commonly charge management and/or performance fees.

INDEXLA provides infrastructure while investors retain control of their assets.

## Does INDEXLA charge management fees?

No.

The initial business model has no management fees.

## Does INDEXLA charge performance fees?

No.

The initial business model has no performance fees.

## Does INDEXLA charge subscription fees?

No.

The initial model is based on execution fees.

## What is the execution fee?

The initial execution fee is 1% on buy and sell transactions executed through INDEXLA.

## Where does the execution fee go?

The distribution depends on the portfolio type.

For protocol-created portfolios:

- 20% Platform
- 30% Treasury
- 40% Rewards Pool
- 10% $DEXLA Buyback & Burn

For creator portfolios:

- 50% Creator
- 20% Platform
- 10% Treasury
- 10% Rewards Pool
- 10% $DEXLA Buyback & Burn

## Why does the creator receive 50%?

Because creators are responsible for bringing investment theses, audiences, and portfolio activity to the marketplace.

The model gives creators a direct economic incentive to build and maintain portfolios that attract investors.

## Why does INDEXLA use execution fees instead of AUM fees?

Execution fees align revenue with actual portfolio activity.

The platform does not need to charge investors a recurring percentage simply for holding assets.

## Can the execution fee change?

The initial model is 1%.

Any future changes would be subject to protocol governance, product requirements, and applicable regulatory considerations.

## What is the Rewards Pool?

The Rewards Pool provides additional incentives for creators, investors, and high-performing portfolios.

It is separate from the creator's 50% execution-fee revenue share.

## Why have a Rewards Pool if creators already earn fees?

The objective is to create additional incentives that encourage creators to remain active, improve portfolio quality, attract capital, and compete for marketplace rewards.

It can also reward investors participating in successful portfolios.

## How are Rewards Pool points calculated?

The initial weighting is:

- Performance: 50%
- AUM Growth: 30%
- Trading Volume: 20%

These metrics can be used to determine rankings and rewards such as the Top 25 portfolio reward program.

## Does the Rewards Pool only reward creators?

No.

The Rewards Pool can reward both creators and investors according to defined reward programs.

## What are the $DEXLA deflationary mechanisms?

There are three:

1. 1,000 $DEXLA burned for creator portfolio publishing.
2. 10% of protocol execution-fee revenue allocated to $DEXLA buyback and burn.
3. 25% of realized Treasury profits allocated to $DEXLA buyback and burn.

## Is the Treasury buyback the same as the execution-fee buyback?

No.

The 10% execution-fee buyback is one mechanism.

The 25% of realized Treasury profits allocated to buyback is a separate mechanism.

## What is the Treasury used for?

The Treasury provides financial resources for:

- Product development
- Operations
- Security
- Infrastructure
- Marketing
- Strategic partnerships
- Ecosystem growth
- Additional community and creator incentives

## Does INDEXLA operate the Treasury as a proprietary trading desk?

No.

The Treasury is primarily a protocol reserve.

Treasury assets may be managed conservatively to preserve capital and maintain liquidity for protocol requirements.

Any permitted treasury investment activity is subject to defined controls and risk management.

## What happens to Treasury profits?

Where permitted treasury activities generate realized profits, 25% of realized profits are allocated to purchasing and permanently burning $DEXLA.

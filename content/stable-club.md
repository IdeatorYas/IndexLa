## **FAQ**

**Where does the yield come from?**  
Trading fees and protocol incentives—not lending interest.

**Does INDEXLA hold my funds?**  
No. Your assets sit inside the selected liquidity pool, and your wallet owns the resulting LP position.

**Can INDEXLA withdraw my assets?**  
INDEXLA can only manage approved positions and return exited assets to your wallet. It cannot transfer them elsewhere.

**What if INDEXLA is unavailable?**  
You still own the LP position and can exit directly through the underlying protocol without using INDEXLA.

**Can I lose money?**  
Yes. Risks include smart-contract exploits, stablecoin depegs, impermanent loss, pool imbalances and market movements. No strategy is risk-free.
